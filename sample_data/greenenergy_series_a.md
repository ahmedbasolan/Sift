# GreenEnergy Corp — Series A Pitch Deck

> **Fictional company, written for the Sift demo.** The company, its people and every figure are invented.

## Company Overview

**Founded:** 2022 | **HQ:** Austin, TX | **Employees:** 28

GreenEnergy Corp develops AI-powered battery management systems for commercial and residential solar installations. Our proprietary firmware extends battery lifespan by 40% compared to industry averages, reducing total cost of ownership for solar-plus-storage deployments.

## Market Opportunity

- Global energy storage market: $44B by 2030 (CAGR 31%)
- Residential solar storage growing 45% YoY
- Current penetration: <5% of eligible homes
- Regulatory tailwinds: IRA tax credits (30% ITC), state-level mandates

## Product

**GridSync OS** — firmware layer that sits between battery hardware and inverter controls.

Key differentiators:
- Predictive degradation modeling using real-time cell data
- Dynamic charge/discharge optimization based on grid pricing
- OTA updates for fleet-wide performance improvements
- Compatible with Tesla Powerwall, Enphase, and BYD batteries

## Business Model

- **SaaS:** $15/month per residential unit, $500/month per commercial site
- **Hardware:** GridSync Edge device ($200/unit, 35% margin)
- **Fleet licensing:** Utility companies pay per-MWh managed
