## Company Overview

**Zerica Inc.**
- **Founded:** 2022 | **HQ:** San Francisco, CA
- **Sector:** Fintech / Embedded Payments
- **Stage:** Series A ($12M raised of $15M round)
- **Employees:** 22

## Problem

SaaS companies spend 3-6 months integrating payment systems (Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly) and lose 20-30% of revenue to payment failures, failed card updates, and involuntary churn. The average SaaS company loses $1.2M/year to preventable payment failures.

**Key pain points:**
- Payment failure rates average 8-12% for subscription businesses
- Card expiry causes 15% of involuntary churn annually
- Existing payment tools require 50-200 hours of engineering to integrate
- No unified view of payment health across multiple processors

## Solution

Zerica's Payment Intelligence Platform sits on top of existing payment processors and:
- **Predicts** payment failures 30 days before they happen using ML models trained on 2B+ transactions
- **Prevents** failures through automatic card updates, retry optimization, and customer outreach
- **Recovers** failed payments with intelligent dunning and alternative payment routing
- **Analyzes** payment health across Stripe, Chargebee, and Braintree in one dashboard

## Product

**Zerica Core** — API-first payment intelligence platform

Key features:
- **Failure Prediction** — ML models predict card declines 30 days ahead (92% accuracy)
- **Smart Retry** — Optimal retry timing increases recovery rates by 35%
- **Card Update Network** — Automatic card-on-file updates via network tokens
- **Payment Analytics** — Real-time dashboard with processor-agnostic insights
- **Dunning Automation** — Personalized recovery sequences based on customer behavior
