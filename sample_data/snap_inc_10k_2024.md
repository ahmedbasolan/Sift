# Snap Inc. — FY2024 Annual Report (10-K Summary)

## Business Overview

Snap Inc. (NYSE: SNAP) is a technology company that builds products to empower people to express themselves, live in the moment, learn about the world, and have fun together. The company's flagship product, Snapchat, is a visual messaging app with a community of over 453 million daily active users.

**Headquarters:** Santa Monica, CA | **Founded:** 2011 | **Employees:** ~5,300
**CEO:** Evan Spiegel | **CFO:** Derek Andersen

## User Metrics

- **Daily Active Users (DAUs):** 453 million in Q4 2024 (+9% YoY, +39 million)
- **DAU Growth:** Sequential and year-over-year growth on both iOS and Android
- **Snap Star Program:** 40% YoY increase in creators posting content
- **Content Engagement:** Total time spent watching Spotlight content increased 175%+ YoY
- **Public Sharing:** Over 1 billion Snaps shared publicly every month

## Geographic Revenue Distribution

- **North America:** Primary revenue market (highest ARPU)
- **Europe:** Growing market with regulatory considerations
- **Rest of World:** Largest user base, lowest ARPU — monetization opportunity

## Key Strategic Initiatives

1. **Augmented Reality** — Spectacles developer platform, AR shopping, Lens Studio ecosystem
2. **Revenue Diversification** — Snapchat+ subscriptions (10M+ subscribers), commerce, Spotlight monetization
3. **Advertising Platform Improvements** — Active advertisers more than doubled in Q4
4. **AI Integration** — My AI chatbot, generative AI features, enhanced ad targeting
5. **Content Ecosystem** — Creator monetization, media partnerships, Spotlight growth

## Risk Factors

1. **Competition** — Intense competition from Meta (Instagram, WhatsApp), TikTok, YouTube for users and advertisers
2. **Revenue Concentration** — Advertising revenue dependent on macroeconomic conditions and advertiser spending
3. **User Growth Deceleration** — Growth rates in North America and Europe may slow as market matures
4. **Regulatory Risk** — Children's online privacy (COPPA), data protection (GDPR), content moderation requirements
5. **AR Investment** — Long-term AR vision requires significant ongoing investment with uncertain returns
6. **Hardware Risk** — Spectacles and AR hardware face technology, manufacturing, and market adoption challenges
7. **Stock-Based Compensation** — Significant SBC creates dilution (FY2024 SBC included in adjusted EBITDA reconciliation)

## Balance Sheet
