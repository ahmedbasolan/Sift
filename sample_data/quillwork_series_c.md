## The Problem

Teams use 5–10 different tools for documentation, project management, wikis and notes. This creates:
- **Information silos** — knowledge scattered across chat, docs, wikis and task trackers
- **Context switching** — knowledge workers move between apps hundreds of times a day
- **Tool sprawl** — teams pay for several overlapping SaaS subscriptions per employee
- **Broken search** — finding one answer means checking several systems

## The Solution

Quillwork is an all-in-one workspace that combines:
- **Docs** — rich text with nested pages, databases and embeds
- **Wiki** — team knowledge base with permissions and templates
- **Projects** — boards, timelines and task management
- **Databases** — structured data with table, board, calendar and gallery views
- **Templates** — 6,000+ community-built templates

**Key insight:** rather than replacing each tool with a better version, replace all of them with one flexible platform.
