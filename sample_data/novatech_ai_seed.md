## Problem

Legal and compliance teams spend 15-20 hours per week manually reviewing contracts, regulatory filings, and policy documents. Current AI solutions produce hallucinated outputs with no source verification, making them unusable for compliance-critical workflows.

## Solution

NovaTech's Document Intelligence Platform uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with source-grounded citations to analyze legal and compliance documents. Every AI output includes verifiable source references with page numbers and trust scoring.

**Key features:**
- Multi-format ingestion (PDF, DOCX, scanned images via OCR)
- Source-grounded analysis with page-level citations
- Compliance checklist automation (SOX, GDPR, HIPAA)
- Team collaboration with audit trails
