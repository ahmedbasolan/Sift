# Meridian Health Systems — FY2025 Annual Report (10-K Summary)

## Business Overview

Meridian Health Systems (NASDAQ: MHS) is a healthcare technology company providing cloud-based electronic health record (EHR) solutions, telehealth platforms, and revenue cycle management services to mid-size hospitals and specialty clinics across the United States.

**Headquarters:** Boston, MA | **Founded:** 2015 | **Employees:** 1,840

## Products & Services

1. **MeridianEHR** — flagship EHR platform serving 340+ hospitals
2. **MeridianTelehealth** — white-label telehealth solution (2.1M visits in FY2025)
3. **MeridianRCM** — AI-assisted revenue cycle management
4. **MeridianAnalytics** — population health and clinical outcomes dashboards

## Risk Factors

1. **Regulatory:** CMS interoperability mandates (CMS-9115-F) require significant platform investment
2. **Competition:** Epic Systems and Cerner dominate large hospital market; niche players (Veracyte, Phreesia) compete in specialty
3. **Cybersecurity:** Healthcare data breaches increased 55% in 2025; a breach could result in significant liability
4. **Customer Concentration:** Top 10 customers represent 28% of revenue
5. **Integration Risk:** Acquired RCM platform (HealthPay360) integration is 6 months behind schedule
6. **Talent:** Competition for healthcare IT engineers drives salary inflation (12% YoY)

## Growth Strategy

- International expansion (Canada, UK pilot programs in FY2026)
- AI clinical decision support (MeridianAI module)
- Vertical integration of RCM and EHR for unified workflow
- Strategic M&A in telehealth and remote patient monitoring

## Outlook FY2026

- Revenue guidance: $168M-$175M (+21-26%)
