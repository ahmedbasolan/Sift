## Business Overview

Shutterbloom Inc. is a listed consumer technology company. Its flagship product, Shutterbloom, is a camera-first messaging app where friends share short-lived photos and videos, with a community of 212 million daily active users.

## Key Strategic Initiatives

1. **Augmented Reality** — camera glasses developer kit, AR shopping try-ons
2. **Revenue Diversification** — grow Shutterbloom Plus and creator monetisation to reduce dependence on advertising
3. **Ad Platform** — new conversion-optimised ad formats; active advertisers up 70% in Q4
4. **AI Features** — in-app assistant and generative camera effects

## Risk Factors

1. **Competition** — larger social and short-video platforms compete for the same users, creators and ad budgets
2. **Advertising Concentration** — 90% of revenue is advertising, which moves with the macroeconomic cycle
3. **Monetisation Gap** — 42% of users are in Rest of World, which brings in 17% of revenue
4. **Regulatory Risk** — minors' online safety rules, data-protection law and content moderation obligations
5. **Hardware Investment** — the camera-glasses line lost $210M in FY2024 with uncertain adoption
6. **Stock-Based Compensation** — SBC of $780M in FY2024 is excluded from Adjusted EBITDA and dilutes shareholders
