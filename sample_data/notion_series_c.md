## The Problem

Teams use 5-10 different tools for documentation, project management, wikis, and notes. This creates:
- **Information silos** — knowledge scattered across Slack, Google Docs, Confluence, Trello, Asana
- **Context switching** — average knowledge worker toggles between apps 1,200 times/day
- **Tool fatigue** — teams spend $5K-15K/year per employee on overlapping SaaS subscriptions
- **Broken search** — finding information requires checking 6+ different systems

## The Solution

Notion is an all-in-one workspace that combines:
- **Docs** — rich text editing with nested pages, databases, and embeds
- **Wiki** — team knowledge base with permissions and templates
- **Projects** — kanban boards, timelines, and task management
- **Databases** — structured data with views (table, board, timeline, calendar, gallery)
- **Templates** — 10,000+ community-built templates for every use case

**Key insight:** Rather than replacing each tool with a better version, replace ALL of them with one flexible platform.
