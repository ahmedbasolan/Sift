# QuickShip Logistics — Due Diligence Brief

> **Fictional company, written for the Sift demo.** The company, its people and every figure are invented.

## Company Profile

**QuickShip Logistics Inc.**
- **Founded:** 2019 | **HQ:** Chicago, IL
- **Sector:** Last-mile delivery / logistics technology
- **Employees:** 420 (down from 580 in Q1)
- **Stage:** Series B ($45M raised, 2022)

## Executive Summary

QuickShip operates a technology-enabled last-mile delivery network across 12 mid-size U.S. markets. The company uses a gig-driver model combined with proprietary route optimization software to offer same-day and next-day delivery for e-commerce retailers.

## Key Concerns

### Revenue Miss
FY2025 actual revenue ($51M) came in 46% below projection ($95M). Management attributed this to:
- Loss of anchor customer (ShopDirect, 22% of revenue) to in-house delivery
- Slower-than-expected market expansion (launched 2 of 6 planned cities)
- Competitive pressure from DoorDash Drive and Uber Direct

### Cash Position
- **Cash on hand:** $8.2M (down from $32M at start of FY2025)
- **Monthly burn:** $5.2M (accelerating despite headcount reduction)
- **Runway:** ~6 weeks at current burn rate
- **Bridge round:** Management is in discussions for $15M convertible note at unfavorable terms (20% discount, 8% interest)

### Unit Economics Deterioration
- **Revenue per delivery:** $4.80 (down from $5.60)
- **Cost per delivery:** $6.20 (up from $5.10)
- **Contribution margin:** -$1.40 per delivery (was +$0.50)
- Drivers are being paid premium rates ($22/hr vs. market $18/hr) to maintain coverage

### Customer Concentration
- Top 3 customers = 61% of revenue
- ShopDirect departure reduced revenue by $15M annually
- Remaining anchor customers (FitGear, PetBox) are evaluating competitors

### Operational Issues
- Driver turnover: 180% annually (industry avg: 120%)
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT): 3.1/5.0 (down from 4.2)
- Late delivery rate: 14% (up from 6%)
- Technology platform: 3 major outages in Q3 2025

## Valuation Context

- **Last valuation:** $180M (Series B, 2022)
- **Current revenue multiple:** 3.5x (vs. peer average 5-8x)
- **Implied current value:** $50-80M (significant down round)
- **Liquidation preference:** 1x non-participating (Series B investors)

## Recommendation

**High Risk — Not Recommended for Investment**

QuickShip faces existential cash flow challenges. The company has burned through 74% of its raised capital with deteriorating unit economics and significant customer concentration risk. The 6-week runway creates urgency that may force unfavorable deal terms.

**Mitigating factors:**
- Technology platform has genuine IP value
- Route optimization patents could be attractive to acquirers
- Potential acquisition target for DoorDash/Uber at distressed valuation

**If proceeding:**
- Require board seat and information rights
- Structure as convertible note with strong downside protection
- Set clear milestones for tranche releases
- Engage operational turnaround consultant
